## What this is

England, Made Fluent is an independent argument about English football: that
the national team's principal limitation is no longer talent, mentality or
togetherness, but **collective fluency**: the trained ability of players
developed in very different tactical systems to understand each other
quickly, under pressure, without losing shared control of the game. The site
sets out that argument in full, compares it against how other footballing
nations have built their own models, and turns it into a set of specific,
testable proposals addressed to the Football Association in good faith.

## Independence

England, Made Fluent is an independent project. It is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or produced in consultation with The Football Association or
the Premier League. Where the site references FA or Premier League
institutions, programmes or history, it does so as an outside observer
working from public material, clearly labelling what is verified fact,
what is still an unverified placeholder source, and what is our own
analytical interpretation. See `/sources/` and `/evidence/` for how that
distinction is maintained throughout.

## Who it is for

This site is written to be readable by an ordinary supporter in a couple of
minutes at its shortest level, while standing up to closer scrutiny from
coaches, journalists, academy staff and anyone inside the FA weighing
whether any of this is worth taking seriously. Most pages offer that choice
directly, through brief, detailed and evidence-level layers of the same
material, so readers can go as deep as they want and stop where they don't.

## What this is not

This site is not a fan rant about a specific manager, player or defeat. It
does not blame individuals for structural problems, and it tries hard to
avoid treating any single tournament result as proof of anything. It is not
a petition and does not ask readers to sign anything. It is not written with
hostility toward the FA. The opposite: its proposals and its open letter
are addressed to the FA as a good-faith contribution, written by people who
want the organisation to succeed. And it is not a consultancy
pitch: nobody involved in this project is offering paid services to the FA,
the Premier League, or any club connected to this argument.

## Corrections

Because this site's credibility depends entirely on how carefully it
handles evidence, corrections are welcome and actively invited: a wrong
date, a mischaracterised source, an interpretation presented without its
label, or a citation that does not actually support the claim attached to
it. Please send corrections to

<a href={`mailto:${"info@gtawyb.com"}`}>info@gtawyb.com</a> with enough detail
(the page, the specific claim, and what you believe is wrong) that it can be
checked quickly.
